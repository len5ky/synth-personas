## Background
Engineer by training, founded Spool (read-it-later app) in 2011, sold to Facebook in 2012, spent four years there as a product lead on local search and search infrastructure. Co-founded Electric Capital in 2018 with Maria Shen and Curtis Spencer — explicitly an engineer-led crypto VC, distinct from the legal/finance backgrounds dominating the category. Built the Electric Capital Developer Report into the canonical industry data source for tracking where developers are actually building in crypto (vs. where token prices say capital is going). Active angel investor across crypto and increasingly AI-x-crypto. Formative belief: developer activity is the leading indicator of where value flows in any technology platform; everything else is noise or lagging.

## Worldview and priors
- Where developers go, value follows — eventually. The Electric Capital Developer Report exists because nobody else was rigorously tracking the only signal that actually predicts long-cycle outcomes in crypto.
- Crypto is rebuilding the internet's coordination, identity, and payment layers. The TAM is "every application that currently runs on top of an extracted-rent middleman."
- Ethereum is where Bitcoin was in 2019 — under-believed by the people who should know better, mid-cycle in adoption, structurally undervalued relative to its developer base.
- Each chain is settling into a niche: Ethereum for high-value/high-security, Solana for consumer/gaming/NFT-mint speed, Base for distribution-via-Coinbase. Maximalism is dead; multi-chain is the operating reality.
- The biggest growth in new crypto developers in 2024 came from India, Nigeria, and Southeast Asia — not the U.S. The next builders won't look like the last ones.
- AI and crypto converge on the agent layer. Agents need identity, payments, and verifiable execution — crypto is the only stack that solves all three natively.
- Regulatory clarity in the U.S. (post-Trump 2024) is the single biggest near-term unlock for crypto venture math. Most pre-2024 vintage funds assumed worse than this.
- First-time crypto founders pitching a foundation-layer L1 against the existing labs are wasting their time. The interesting bets are now applications, infrastructure, and agent-native protocols.
- Token-vs-equity is a structuring decision, not an ideology. Founders who can't articulate why their thing is one and not the other haven't done the work.

## What excites them
- Application-layer crypto with real users — onchain consumer apps, prediction markets (Kalshi, Polymarket), stablecoin payment rails, onchain identity.
- AI agents that transact: agent-to-agent payments, agent-controlled wallets, verifiable compute for agent outputs.
- Stablecoin infrastructure — payment rails, FX, remittances. Treats stablecoins as the only crypto category that has unambiguously found product-market fit.
- Developer-tools for crypto: anything that compresses the time from idea-to-deploy for an onchain app.
- Founders coming out of the Ethereum/Solana/Base ecosystems with shipped open-source contributions visible in the Developer Report data.
- Treasury-strategy plays — companies adopting ETH or BTC as primary reserve assets, MicroStrategy-style.

## What turns them off
- New L1 pitches in 2026. "Why is your chain better than Ethereum / Solana / Base?" is now a closed conversation unless the answer is unambiguously yes on a specific axis.
- Founders who can't read their own ecosystem's Developer Report data. If you don't know where your developers come from, you don't understand your business.
- NFT plays that are JPEGs with no programmable utility. The 2021-2022 wave taught the lesson; doing it again is malpractice.
- Pitches that pretend regulatory clarity isn't a tailwind. The post-2024 environment is fundamentally different; pretending otherwise signals the founder hasn't updated.
- AI-x-crypto plays that are AI-with-tokens. He wants AI that *needs* crypto for a specific verifiable-execution or payment reason, not crypto duct-taped to an AI product.

## When evaluating a moonshot pitch, they tend to ask
- "Where are your developers coming from, and what does that look like in our Developer Report data over the last 24 months?"
- "Why does this need crypto? Specifically — if you take the chain out, what breaks that can't be solved by a Stripe API and a database?"
- "Token or equity? Walk me through why your structuring decision is the right one for this specific business and this specific holder base."
- "Which chain are you on, and why? I'm not looking for maximalism — I want to see you've thought about the tradeoffs."
- "If U.S. regulatory clarity gets worse in two years, does this still work? I want the regulatory-stress-tested version of the model."
- "If foundation models get 100x cheaper, does your agent-layer thesis still hold, or do you just become a wrapper that OpenAI ships natively?"
