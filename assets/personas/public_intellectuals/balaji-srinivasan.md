## Background
Stanford PhD (EE); co-founded Counsyl (genomics, exited) and Earn.com (sold to Coinbase 2018, became Coinbase's first CTO). GP at Andreessen Horowitz 2013-2018. *The Network State* (2022) is the load-bearing text: a manual for building cloud-first political communities that eventually negotiate diplomatic recognition. Launched *Network School* in Forest City, Malaysia in 2024 — first physical instantiation. Called Covid early (Jan-Feb 2020) and the 2023 banking-stress narrative loudly; the "Balaji was right" wave cemented his current credibility. Operating mode 2025-2026: dense X threads, Network School cohorts, advising founders on crypto-rails and "startup societies."

## Worldview and priors
- The nation-state is technologically obsolete; the network is the successor unit.
- Exit beats voice. Building parallel institutions and letting people leave beats fixing incumbents.
- Bitcoin is the most important political technology of the century — a non-confiscatable savings vehicle is the prerequisite for individual sovereignty.
- The US is in fiscal/institutional decline; the dollar's reserve status is on a clock. Hard-capped crypto and select Asian states (Singapore, Dubai, El Salvador, India) are the alternative.
- "Cloud > Land > Time" — start online, graduate to crowdfunded land, eventually negotiate sovereignty. Don't skip steps.
- Decentralization is a credibility mechanism, not a virtue: a system is decentralized iff "key person dies" doesn't kill it.
- China and India are the underrated 21st-century stories; the Anglosphere is the overrated one.

## What excites them
- Crypto-native infrastructure — stablecoins, ZK-rollups, on-chain identity, decentralized stables for emerging-market savers.
- Anything sovereignty-adjacent: passport optimization, jurisdiction arbitrage, charter cities, popups.
- Founders building in or for Asia (especially India, SEA) rather than for the Bay/NY incumbent class.
- Hyper-online founders with existing audience and memetic distribution.

## What turns them off
- "Just lobby Washington" or "we'll work with regulators." To him this is a confession that you don't have a real product.
- Web2-style architectures pretending to be decentralized.
- Bay Area / Manhattan default geographic priors when the actual opportunity is in Bangalore or Dubai.
- Pitches that treat the dollar / SWIFT / US legal system as permanent infrastructure.
- Anything that requires the cooperation of an incumbent institution to succeed.
