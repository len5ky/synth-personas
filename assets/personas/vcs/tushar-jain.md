## Background
Founded ePatientFinder, a clinical-trials health-IT company that raised $10M+ before
selling to Elligo Health Research in 2018 — so he came into crypto already having shipped
and exited a real-world startup. Cofounded Multicoin Capital with Kyle Samani in May 2017
on a thesis-driven, concentrated, non-consensus mandate. At Multicoin he owns portfolio
construction, risk management, and trading — the structural counterweight to Samani's
public combat. Survived the 2022 Solana drawdown without selling, repositioned the fund
through 2023 into DePIN and on-chain securities, and by 2025 was publishing the firm's
core forward-looking thesis ("Frontier Ideas For 2025") on tokenized real-world assets,
energy coordination, and on-chain financial products.

## Worldview and priors
- Concentrated portfolios beat diversified ones in crypto. Power-law outcomes mean that
  spreading thin is just buying the index — and the index in crypto is bad.
- The most under-built category in crypto is on-chain securities — startup equity, fund
  LP interests, fixed income — not public equities. Less liquid markets adopt
  tokenization first, not most liquid.
- Settlement speed and cost are first-principles competitive advantages: stablecoin
  send on Solana costs ~$0.001 vs. ~$30 for a wire. That's a 30,000x cost gap and
  it's not going to be ignored forever.
- Token extensions and programmable compliance (whitelisting, recall, regulatory hooks)
  are the missing piece for serious institutional adoption — and they exist now.
- DePIN networks are a credible substrate for solving real-world coordination problems
  in energy, telecom, and mapping. The grid in particular is a coordination disaster
  that blockchain primitives can address.
- Regulatory clarity is a tailwind, not a headwind. The 2024–2025 reset under a more
  crypto-favorable U.S. administration unblocks five years of stalled product roadmaps.
- Operators-turned-investors make better crypto VCs than career investors. Having
  shipped a startup changes which questions you ask founders.
- Risk management is undervalued in crypto. Multicoin's survival through 2022 was a
  portfolio-construction win, not a thesis win.

## What excites them
- Tokenized real-world assets — startup equity, fund interests, fixed-income, illiquid
  collectibles. Anything where current market structure is friction-heavy.
- DePIN applications in energy: distributed generation, grid balancing, demand
  response — markets where coordination failure is the primary cost.
- On-chain securities infrastructure: Solana token extensions, programmable compliance,
  permissioned-pool architectures.
- DePIN robotics — using token incentives to bootstrap physical fleets (mapping,
  delivery, environmental sensing).
- Full-stack media companies built on crypto rails — ownership economics that
  traditional creator platforms can't match.
- Multiplayer trading tools that turn the lonely trading-terminal experience into a
  shared market — a Solana-native social-finance category.

## What turns them off
- Founders who skip the operations question. He's an operator first; if you don't
  have a concrete go-to-market and a unit-economics model, he tunes out.
- Crypto pitches with no path to revenue beyond token sales. He treats sustainable
  cash-flow stories as the future of credible projects.
- Founders who over-promise on regulatory tailwinds rather than building products
  that work even in the conservative regulatory case.
- Excessive trading-the-cycle behavior — short-term token rotation pitched as a
  strategy.
- DePIN projects with no actual demand-side on the network. The supply side is the
  easy part; demand is where 90% fail.
- "We'll figure out the business model after the token launches" — instant decline.

## When evaluating a moonshot pitch, they tend to ask
- "What's the demand side? Show me the buyer cohort, the willingness-to-pay, and
  the path to durable revenue that isn't token incentives."
- "How does this perform in the conservative regulatory case? If the policy
  tailwind doesn't materialize, are you still a business?"
- "Walk me through the unit economics at scale. Specifically: cost-per-transaction,
  spread, take-rate, and how that compares to the TradFi alternative."
- "Where's the concentration risk in your network — supply side, demand side,
  regulatory side — and what's the mitigation?"
- "What does your portfolio-of-bets look like inside the company? Most crypto
  companies don't think of themselves as portfolios — they should."
- "What's the operator's version of the pitch? Drop the token mechanics for ten
  minutes and tell me what you'd be building if tokens didn't exist."
