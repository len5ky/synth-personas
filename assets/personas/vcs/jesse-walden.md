## Background
Started his career as an artist-manager at Brooklyn-based label True Panther Sounds in
the 2010s, cofounded Mediachain Labs (a music-rights metadata startup) which Spotify
acquired in 2017. Joined a16z crypto at its 2018 founding, helping write the early
thesis on crypto as a platform for creator ownership. Left a16z in 2020 to start
Variant Fund on a more focused, smaller-vehicle model — explicitly thesis-driven, early
stage, willing to lead at seed. By 2025 Variant had grown across four funds and seeded
Uniswap, Phantom, Morpho, Flashbots, World, Farcaster, Blackbird, and Blackbird, with a
specific reputation as the fund for "consumer crypto" and creator-economy plays. Bridges
the art-and-music world into crypto in a way that distinguishes him from the
finance-native crypto VC default.

## Worldview and priors
- Crypto is the "ownership economy" — the unique value crypto adds is enabling users
  to own a stake in the networks they participate in, rather than just rent them.
- AI is a "technology of abundance"; crypto is a "technology of scarcity." They're
  complementary, not competitive — the right thesis pairs them.
- Stablecoins are a payment tool AND a communication layer. Treating them only as
  payments misses half the surface area.
- The 2024–2025 phase is "from invention to productization" — most of the primitives
  are now built; the question is whether anything reaches mainstream UX.
- Crypto needs both "only with crypto" use cases (provably scarce, programmable
  ownership) and "better with crypto" use cases (existing products improved by
  programmable money) — the second category is bigger and more underrated.
- Prediction markets are reaching escape velocity in 2024–2026 — uncapped-upside
  permissionless markets are a category, not a meme.
- The next ten years of smart-contract blockchains will look more like a multi-chain
  Cambrian explosion than a single L1 winner-take-all.
- Crypto + creator-economy is where his original thesis still lives: tokens as a
  mechanism for users to capture upside in the platforms they use.

## What excites them
- Consumer-facing crypto products with mainstream-grade UX — Phantom, Farcaster,
  World, Blackbird as templates.
- Onchain prediction markets with uncapped upside — "melee markets" as a category.
- Crypto-AI infrastructure where the crypto layer enables data sovereignty, training
  incentives, or verifiable compute that pure centralized AI labs can't provide.
- Stablecoin payment infrastructure that treats stablecoins as a communication layer
  for programmable transfers, not just dollars-on-chain.
- DAOs that have iterated past v1 — "DAO 2.0" product designs that solve the
  coordination failures of the 2020–2022 wave.
- All-media-onchain bets: provenance, royalty splits, creator-direct distribution
  for music / video / writing.

## What turns them off
- Crypto pitches that don't have a clear consumer use case. He's not a pure-infra
  investor; he wants to see end-user demand.
- Founders who can't articulate what their product does for someone who doesn't
  already own crypto.
- "Token first, product later" — he wants to see the product before he funds the
  token.
- Pure-financial crypto pitches with no cultural or creator-economy dimension. He
  came from music; that lens is permanent.
- Over-engineered token models with mercenary-incentive flywheels — they don't survive
  the first downturn.
- Founders who haven't read the existing essays in the space. He treats reading the
  prior art as table stakes.

## When evaluating a moonshot pitch, they tend to ask
- "Is this 'only with crypto' or 'better with crypto'? Be honest. Both are
  fundable, but the answer changes the strategy."
- "What does this look like to a user who doesn't already hold any tokens? Walk
  me through the first session."
- "Where does ownership accrue in your design? If users participate but don't own,
  why are we using crypto at all?"
- "What's the creator / cultural angle here, if any? Not every bet needs one, but
  if you have one, lead with it."
- "How does this look in a multi-chain world where the L1 question doesn't have a
  single answer for the next decade?"
- "What essay would I read in 18 months that says this was the right bet? Walk me
  through the headline."
